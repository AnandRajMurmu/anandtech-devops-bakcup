# Section 00 — Computer and IT Prerequisites — PLAN

**Document type:** Mutable section planning record

**Status:** Active discussion

**Authority:** Non-authoritative; the section SSOT becomes authoritative only after explicit owner lock

## Accepted direction carried into this draft

- Ten-unit section structure.
- Absolute-beginner starting point.
- Feynman-style explanations that build accurate mental models before terminology or commands.
- Mini-book depth with concrete examples, progressive practice, evidence interpretation, troubleshooting, production connections, and assessments.
- Environment-independent explanations and safe optional local inspection; no VM requirement in this section.
- No learner-facing generation before the section SSOT is explicitly locked.

## Design decisions represented in the SSOT draft

1. The section moves from hardware execution to operating systems, running work, memory, persistence, identity, communication, applications, the command line, and evidence-based troubleshooting.
2. Unit 10 integrates the preceding mental models instead of introducing a new tool stack.
3. The cumulative artifact is `first_investigation_notes.md`; small unit evidence entries build toward it.
4. Commands, where useful, are optional observation exercises and must declare their platform assumptions.
5. Virtual machines, Linux administration depth, cloud accounts, containers, CI/CD tools, and persistent lab architecture remain deferred to their owning sections.
